# ETF screen — concentration-capped & mid-cap alternatives
Date: 2026-06-03 · Researcher: bb8 subagent
Disclaimer: data is subject to change; not investment advice. TER, AUM and methodology should be re-checked on the issuer's KIID/PRIIPs document before any trade.

## How to read the tables
- TER = total expense ratio; lower is better.
- Replication: ✅ physical / ❌ synthetic.
- Distribution: Acc = accumulating, Dist = distributing.
- Top-N concentration is the key metric here — quoted from issuer factsheet at most-recent month-end.
- "Cap mechanism" describes HOW the heavy-weight problem is mitigated.

**bb8 take:** Switzerland is structurally limited for accumulating UCITS — nearly all Swiss-listed CH-domiciled funds distribute. The SLI (via CSSLI or XSLI) is the most direct "capped SMI" answer: it mechanically limits Nestlé+Roche+Novartis to max 27% combined vs. 50%+ in SMI. The SMIM (UBS at 0.25% or iShares at 0.45%) eliminates the big-3 entirely. Graham already holds SPMCHA which IS the UBS SMIM — he's already positioned here. The SLI might be the better complement since it keeps blue-chip exposure but capped. The distributing-only caveat is unavoidable for Swiss-domiciled funds; the withholding tax reclaim is straightforward for Swiss residents.

## Cross-region shortlist

For a Swiss investor wanting low-TER, physical, accumulating, concentration-capped exposure:

1. **Xtrackers S&P 500 Equal Weight (XDEW)** — 0.15% TER, €9bn AUM, mechanically reduces each S&P 500 name to 0.20%. The single best Mag-7 dilution tool for US exposure.
2. **Invesco MSCI World Equal Weight (MWEQ)** — 0.20% TER, global equal-weight in one fund. If Graham simplifies to one world ETF with deconcentration, this is it.
3. **iShares MSCI Europe Mid Cap (EUMD)** — 0.15% TER, broad Europe mid-cap without ASML/SAP/LVMH mega-caps. Cheapest European deconcentration.
4. **Xtrackers MSCI World ex USA (EXUS)** — 0.15% TER, structural US-zero allocation if Graham wants to pair a US EW fund with a non-US complement.
5. **Xtrackers MSCI EM ex China (XDEX)** — 0.16% TER, cleanly removes China mega-caps; pairs well with existing EM allocation.
6. **SPDR S&P 400 US Mid Cap (SPY4)** — 0.30% TER, pure US mid-cap with zero mega-cap overlap; listed on SIX in CHF.
7. **Xtrackers SLI (XSLI)** — 0.25% TER, caps Nestlé/Roche/Novartis at 9% each. Only available distributing, but the cheapest SLI ETF and reduces Swiss concentration meaningfully.
